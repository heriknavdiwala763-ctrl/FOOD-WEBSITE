The Art Of Fiery Molcajete Salsa

At Mexican Heat, salsa isn’t just a side dip — it is the heartbeat of our restaurant. Inspired by age-old Oaxaca kitchens, we fire-roast red Roma tomatoes, serrano peppers, charred jalapeños, and smoky chipotles directly over roaring flame griddles (comal).

Our master chefs hand-crush each batch inside authentic volcanic basalt rock bowls (molcajetes) with sea salt, garlic, fresh lime juice, and mountain cilantro. This ancient technique extracts rich aromatic essential oils that no modern food processor can ever match.
